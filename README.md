# Ko Olina CRB Smart Containment Pilot — demonstration dashboard

Public, non-real-time demonstration of the pilot's dashboard, published by Ulu Lāhui Foundation for the Ko Olina Community Association and the Office of Councilmember Andria Tupola.

**Every "confirmed" detection in this build is a synthetic example**: a real frame from the pilot's cameras with an openly licensed iNaturalist photograph of *Oryctes rhinoceros* composited in, scored by the real detector. They exist to show how the workflow looks. No coconut rhinoceros beetle has been confirmed on camera at Ko Olina as of the build date shown in the header. Photo counts, weather, camera health and locations (rounded) are real. Imagery of real frames and parcel-level detail are withheld per the pilot's data-handling agreement.

Live site: https://ulu-lahui-foundation.github.io/crb-pilot-demo/

Beetle photographs: iNaturalist contributors, CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-NC; attribution list held with the source data. Base map: Esri World Imagery, OpenStreetMap contributors.
